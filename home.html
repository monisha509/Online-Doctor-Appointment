About The System

Accessible Healthcare Management for Modern Patients

This online doctor appointment management portal streamlines outpatient flow, minimizes in-clinic waiting room crowding, and ensures every patient can reserve a dedicated consultation time with their preferred specialist.

Built as an academic and portfolio demonstration for Rajalakshmi Engineering College, the system emphasizes safe user inputs, responsive layouts, data persistence via localStorage, and clear patient communication.
